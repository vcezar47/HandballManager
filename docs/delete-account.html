Account deletion

Delete your Handball Manager account

Handball Manager  ·  by Cezar-Mihai Vieru-Potecu  ·  App package com.handballmanager.mobile

You only have an account if you signed in to play the Weekly Challenge or the Country Battle. Your careers in the three save slots live on your phone and are never part of an account.

01In the app

Open the app, go to Main menu → your profile, and tap Delete my online account. Confirm, and your account is deleted at once.

02Without the app

Email vieru.potecu@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Handball Manager account” and your username in the game. We will confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner.

03What is deleted

  • Your online account and the Google account identifier it was linked to
  • Your profile: username, nationality, picture, achievement title and showcase season card
  • Reports you made, and reports about you
  • All your Weekly Challenge attempts, their game logs and your leaderboard entries
  • All your Country Battle careers, their game logs and the points they earned your country

Nothing is kept, except that nightly server backups hold a copy for up to 14 days before it is gone from them too. Your careers on your phone are not affected; remove them by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.
