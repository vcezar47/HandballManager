Privacy Policy

Handball Manager

Last updated 21 October 2026  ·  App package com.handballmanager.mobile

Your careers are saved on your phone and stay there. If you choose to play online (the Weekly Challenge and the Country Battle), you sign in with Google and the game sends what you do in those online modes to our server, so it can check every score and keep the leaderboards fair. The app also shows ads, and shares a device advertising identifier with Google to do that.

This policy explains, in plain terms, how the Handball Manager mobile app ("the app", "we") handles your information: what stays on your phone, what reaches our server and why, who else is involved, how long anything is kept, and how to have it deleted.

01Who is responsible

Handball Manager is made by Cezar-Mihai Vieru-Potecu, an independent developer, who is the controller of the personal data described here. You can reach him at vieru.potecu@gmail.com.

02Data that stays on your device

Your saved careers, teams, settings and achievements are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, and nobody else can see them. Season cards you save go to your phone’s gallery, and you choose where to share them.

You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the app.

03Playing online: your account

The online modes are optional. Everything else in the game works without an account. If you choose to play online, you sign in with your Google account and we create an online account for you.

What we keep

  • Google account identifier: a number Google gives us to recognise your sign-in. We do not receive or keep your email address, your name or your Google profile.
  • The profile you set up: your username, the nationality you pick, and optionally a profile picture, the achievement title you have earned (you can hide it), and one season card to showcase.
  • Your Country Battle country: the country you choose to play for.
  • When your account was created and last used.

What other players can see

Your username, picture, nationality, achievement title (if shown), showcase season card, and your positions and scores on the Weekly Challenge and Country Battle leaderboards are public to other players of the game. Don’t use your real name or a photo of yourself unless you are happy for others to see it.

04Weekly Challenge and Country Battle

In the two online modes, the game records the decisions you make in that run or battle career, such as each day advanced, transfers, team sheets and match instructions, together with short fingerprints of the game world, and sends this game log to our server. The server replays the log to check that your result really came from playing the game, and only then puts your score on a leaderboard or credits your points to your country. This protects every player from cheating.

The game log describes what happened in the game. It contains no personal information beyond being linked to your account. A log may also contain short technical notes when something went wrong, such as a failed save, to help us find faults.

Your normal careers in the three save slots are never sent anywhere.

05Reports and moderation

You can report another player’s username or picture. We keep the report (who reported whom, what, and an optional note) to review it. A picture reported by several players is hidden until it is reviewed, and accounts that break the rules can be banned.

06Crash and error reports (testers only)

Players taking part in our test programme may have crash and error reports sent to our server automatically. These contain the device model, Android version, app version and the technical error messages, and are used only to fix faults. Players outside the test programme send no such reports.

07Advertising

The app shows full-screen ads supplied by Google AdMob. AdMob collects a device advertising identifier, a resettable ID your phone assigns for advertising, together with technical information such as your device type, your approximate region derived from your internet connection, and which ads you were shown. Google processes this under its own privacy policy. AdMob does not receive your saved games, your online account or anything you do in the game.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised ad is requested, using Google’s certified consent message. You are free to refuse, and you can change your choice later from the main menu. Refusing does not restrict any part of the game; you will simply see ads that are not personalised.

You can reset your advertising identifier, or switch off ad personalisation, in your device’s settings, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

08Purchases

The optional “remove ads” purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made, and never see your payment details.

10Where your data is kept, and who helps us

Our server runs on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany, inside the European Union. Oracle hosts the server for us and does not use its contents for its own purposes.

The other services involved are from Google: sign-in, Google Play distribution and purchases, and AdMob advertising, each under Google’s Privacy Policy. Your internet address is used to deliver each request and to limit abuse, but our server does not keep it.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

11How long we keep it

  • Your online account, profile, game logs, scores and Country Battle points are kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Reports are kept until they are resolved, and while they are needed to handle repeated abuse.
  • Testers’ crash and error reports are deleted once the fault is dealt with.
  • Nightly backups of the server are kept for 14 days, so deleted data is gone from the backups within 14 days.

12Deleting your account, and your rights

You can delete your online account at any time in the app: Main menu → your profile → Delete my online account. This immediately deletes your account, profile, picture, the reports made by or about you, all your Weekly Challenge attempts and game logs, and all your Country Battle careers, logs and points. Your careers on your phone are not touched. If you no longer have the app, you can ask for deletion by email (see how to delete your account).

Under data protection law, including the GDPR, you have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to receive a copy of it, to object to its use, and to withdraw any consent you have given. To use any of these rights, email us. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

13Children’s privacy

The app is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. The online modes need a Google account, which Google offers only to people old enough to have one in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has created an online account, contact us and we will delete it.

14Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when a feature that handles data is added, we will update this page and revise the “last updated” date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s update notes.
